# Batho

**Codebase Intelligence for AI-Powered Development**

Batho turns your codebase into structured, queryable knowledge optimized for LLMs, agents, and developer tooling.

---

## 🚀 What is Batho?

Batho analyzes your codebase and converts it into a **structured graph of entities and relationships**, then compresses it into formats that AI systems can actually understand.

Instead of raw files, you get:

* Functions, classes, variables
* Relationships (calls, imports, usage)
* A compressed representation for LLMs
* Versioned snapshots of your codebase

---

## ⚡ Quick Start

```bash
pip install batho

# Index your project
batho index --root .

# Generate AI-ready output
batho bsg --root . --mode compressed --budget 12000

# Track changes
batho patch --root . --scan
```

| Feature                | Benefit                                  |
